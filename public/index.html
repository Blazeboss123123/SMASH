Movement. Three jumps. Touch a wall in the air to cling and slide; jump to leap off it (hold up to climb straight up) — walls give your jumps back. Shield + a direction on the ground is a dash; in the air it is a dodge. Down in the air fast-falls, down + jump drops through thin platforms.
Combos. Any attack that lands can be cancelled into a jump, a dash or a different attack — chain up to three. A dodge with no direction in the air lets you throw a ground attack mid-air. Shield just before a hit lands to parry. After a grab: attack pummels, a direction throws.
